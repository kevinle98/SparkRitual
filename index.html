About SparkRitual

Made by players, for players.

SparkRitual is a Commander deck builder from people who sleeve up every week. We built the tool we wanted at our own tables: fast card search that knows your commander's color identity, an honest bracket estimate before Rule Zero talks, and test hands to see how a deck opens.

There's no paywall and no ads. Brew, tinker, share your decks, and get back to playing.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

SparkRitual collects as little as it needs to save and share your decks. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Without an account

Your decks and preferences (such as theme, view and sort order) are stored in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you sign in.

With an account

When you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your email address, a unique account ID and, where available, your name from that provider. We store your account, your display name, and the decks you save, including their names, descriptions, card lists and visibility settings.

Your display name and any decks you set to Unlisted or Public can be seen by other people. Private decks, and everything when your account's deck visibility is set to Hidden, are visible only to you.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Google and Discord confirm your identity when you sign in with them.
  • Scryfall receives your card searches directly from your browser and serves card data and images.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve fonts and a code library to your browser.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can change your display name and deck visibility in Account Settings, delete decks at any time, and sign out to stop syncing. To have your account and all its data deleted, contact us at privacy@sparkritual.io.
