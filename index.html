About SparkRitual

Made by players, for players.

SparkRitual is a Commander deck builder from people who sleeve up every week. We built the tool we wanted at our own tables: fast card search that knows your commander's color identity, an honest bracket estimate before Rule Zero talks, and test hands to see how a deck opens.

There's no paywall and no ads. Brew, tinker, share your decks, and get back to playing.
